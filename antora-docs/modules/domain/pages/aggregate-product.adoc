== Responsibility

`Product` represents a catalog item. It holds product identity, classification, price and global alert threshold.
Stock belongs to `StorageLocation` through `StockLevel`; movements belong to `StockMovement`.

== Attributes

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Attribute | Type | Description

| `productId`
| `ProductId`
| System-generated, immutable unique identifier.

| `name`
| `String`
| Commercial name used for search, for example "Toyota Corolla oil filter".

| `reference`
| `String`
| Technical reference, unique in the catalog, for example "FH-TOY-2024-001".

| `categoryId`
| `CategoryId`
| Reference to the product's single category.

| `minimumGlobalThreshold`
| `int`
| Alert threshold for combined shop-floor and backstock quantities. Falling below it raises `LowStockAlert`.

| `unitPrice`
| `Money`
| Unit selling price, including amount and currency.
|===

== Invariants

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Invariant | Description

| Nonempty `name`
| A product must have a name.

| Unique `reference`
| Checked by the infrastructure repository, not by the aggregate.

| `minimumGlobalThreshold` ≥ 0
| The threshold cannot be negative.

| `unitPrice` > 0
| The unit price must be strictly positive.
|===

== Behavior

`isGlobalStockBelowThreshold(globalQuantity)` compares a supplied global quantity to the threshold.
A Domain Service or Application Service calculates the quantity from storage locations and coordinates the check.

`Product` contains no stock levels, movements, sales or storage locations. These relationships are represented by identifiers in other aggregates.
