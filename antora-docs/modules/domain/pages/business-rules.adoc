== Aggregate invariants

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Rule | Description | On violation

| R-PROD-01
| Product name is nonempty.
| Reject creation/update.

| R-PROD-02
| Product reference is unique in the catalog.
| Repository rejects creation.

| R-PROD-03
| Global alert threshold ≥ 0.
| Reject update.

| R-PROD-04
| Unit price > 0.
| Reject creation/update.

| R-LOC-01
| StockLevel quantity ≥ 0.
| `InsufficientStockException`.

| R-LOC-02
| Location label is nonempty.
| Reject creation.

| R-LOC-03
| Shop-floor low-stock indicator ≥ 0.
| Reject update.

| R-MOV-01
| Movement quantity > 0.
| Reject creation.

| R-MOV-02
| ENTRY: source is null; destination is required.
| `InvalidMovementException`.

| R-MOV-03
| EXIT: source is required; destination is null.
| `InvalidMovementException`.

| R-MOV-04
| TRANSFER: distinct source and destination are required.
| `InvalidMovementException`.

| R-MOV-05
| Movements cannot change after creation.
| No setters exposed.

| R-SALE-01
| At least one SaleLineItem.
| Reject creation.

| R-SALE-02
| Each line quantity > 0.
| Reject line creation.

| R-SALE-03
| Each line unit price > 0.
| Reject line creation.

| R-SALE-04
| totalAmount equals the sum of lineTotal values.
| Calculated automatically.
|===

== Cross-aggregate business rules

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Rule | Description

| R-ALLOC-01
| Allocate from `SHOP_FLOOR` before `BACKSTOCK`.

| R-ALLOC-02
| Reject the sale if total stock across locations is insufficient.

| R-ALLOC-03
| Propose the allocation to the seller for confirmation before execution.

| R-RECV-01
| The user decides how received stock is distributed across locations.

| R-RECV-02
| Create one receipt movement per target location.

| R-RECV-03
| Create an unknown product before recording its receipt.

| R-ALERT-01
| Raise `LowStockAlert` when global stock < `minimumGlobalThreshold`.

| R-ALERT-02
| Raise `ShopFloorLow` when shop-floor stock < `lowStockIndicator`.

| R-ALERT-03
| Clear the critical alert when global stock rises above the threshold (`StockReplenished`).
|===

== Initial TDD test plan

The original test plan prioritizes these invariants:

. `StorageLocation`: successful decrease with sufficient stock; rejection below zero; creation of a missing stock level on increase.
. `StockMovement`: reject ENTRY with a source, EXIT with a destination, TRANSFER with identical locations, and nonpositive quantities.
. `Sale`: reject empty sales and calculate the correct total.
. `StockAllocationService`: shop-floor-only allocation, mixed shop-floor/backstock allocation, and insufficient-stock rejection.
. `Product`: check `isGlobalStockBelowThreshold` on both sides of the threshold.
