The ubiquitous language is the shared vocabulary used by developers and domain experts.
Each term has one meaning within this system.

== Domain terms

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Term | Definition | DDD type

| Product (`Product`)
| Catalog item, such as an oil filter or spark plug, identified by name and unique reference.
| Aggregate Root

| Category (`Category`)
| Classification such as filters, oils or engine parts. Each product belongs to one category.
| Aggregate Root

| Storage location (`StorageLocation`)
| Physical location holding products: shop floor or backstock.
| Aggregate Root

| Shop floor (`SHOP_FLOOR`)
| Customer-facing sales area, used first when allocating a sale.
| `LocationType` value

| Backstock (`BACKSTOCK`)
| Rear storage area holding goods before transfer to the shop floor.
| `LocationType` value

| Stock level (`StockLevel`)
| Quantity of a product at a location; never negative.
| Value Object

| Stock movement (`StockMovement`)
| Immutable record of a receipt, issue or transfer.
| Aggregate Root

| Receipt (`ENTRY`)
| Goods enter a location from an external supplier. No internal source.
| `MovementType` value

| Issue (`EXIT`)
| Goods leave a location, usually for a `Sale`. No internal destination.
| `MovementType` value

| Transfer (`TRANSFER`)
| Internal movement between locations; global stock is unchanged.
| `MovementType` value

| Sale (`Sale`)
| Commercial transaction recording products sold to a customer, composed of line items.
| Aggregate Root

| Sale line (`SaleLineItem`)
| Product, quantity, unit price and line total within a sale.
| Internal `Sale` entity

| Global alert threshold (`minimumGlobalThreshold`)
| Minimum combined shop-floor/backstock quantity below which an alert is raised.
| `Product` attribute

| Shop-floor low-stock indicator (`lowStockIndicator`)
| Quantity below which the shop floor needs replenishment from backstock; not a critical alert.
| `StorageLocation` attribute

| Global stock
| Sum of a product's `StockLevel` quantities across all locations; a calculated view.
| Derived concept

| Shop (`Shop`)
| Physical establishment containing one or more storage locations; supports multiple shops.
| Aggregate Root

| Allocation
| Automatic distribution of a sale request across available locations, using the shop floor first.
| Domain Service concept
|===

== Terminology clarification

The customer sometimes uses "stock" to mean only backstock.
In this system, "stock" means the combined shop-floor and backstock quantity.
Use "backstock quantity" or the `StockLevel` of a `BACKSTOCK` location when referring only to rear storage.
