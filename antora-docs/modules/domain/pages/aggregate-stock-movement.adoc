== Responsibility

`StockMovement` is an immutable stock audit record. Each movement records one atomic operation: product, quantity, source and/or destination, user and timestamp.
A movement is created once and is never updated or deleted.

== Attributes

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Attribute | Type | Description

| `movementId`
| `MovementId`
| Unique movement identifier.

| `productId`
| `ProductId`
| Product concerned by the movement.

| `sourceLocationId`
| `LocationId?`
| Source location; `null` for `ENTRY`, whose source is outside the system.

| `destinationLocationId`
| `LocationId?`
| Destination location; `null` for `EXIT`, whose destination is outside the system (the customer).

| `type`
| `MovementType`
| `ENTRY`, `EXIT` or `TRANSFER`.

| `quantity`
| `int`
| Units moved, strictly positive.

| `performedBy`
| `UserId`
| User who performed the movement.

| `occurredAt`
| `DateTime`
| Movement timestamp.

| `saleId`
| `SaleId?`
| Sale reference, present only for sale-related `EXIT` movements.
|===

== Invariants and factory methods

Every movement has a strictly positive quantity. Static factory methods enforce the source and destination rules:

* `createEntry(productId, destinationId, quantity, userId)` sets `sourceLocationId = null` and `type = ENTRY`; a destination is required.
* `createExit(productId, sourceId, quantity, userId, saleId)` requires a source, sets `destinationLocationId = null` and `type = EXIT`. A sale-related exit requires `saleId`.
* `createTransfer(productId, sourceId, destinationId, quantity, userId)` requires distinct source and destination locations and sets `type = TRANSFER`.

There are no setters or other mutation methods.

== Aggregate boundary

Movements are queried and aggregated independently of products, for example by date or location.
Keeping them separate prevents unbounded product aggregates and avoids locking an entire product when sellers create movements concurrently.

== Typical repository queries

The infrastructure `StockMovementRepository`, rather than the aggregate, handles:

* Today's movements: `findByDate(today)`.
* Product movements: `findByProductId(productId)`.
* Location movements: `findByLocationId(locationId)`.
* Sale movements: `findBySaleId(saleId)`.
* Date ranges: `findByDateRange(from, to)`.
