== Responsibility

`Sale` represents a sales transaction. The aggregate is created atomically when the seller confirms the basket.
It contains `SaleLineItem` entries describing the products, quantities and prices sold.
The sale records the commercial transaction; linked `StockMovement` records provide physical traceability.

== Creation input: SaleLineRequest

Creation accepts a list of immutable `SaleLineInput` DTOs/records to protect the internal entity's encapsulation.

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Attribute | Type | Description

| `productId`
| `ProductId`
| Product to sell.

| `quantity`
| `int`
| Quantity requested by the seller.

| `unitPrice`
| `Money`
| Unit price when the request is made.
|===

== Sale attributes

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Attribute | Type | Description

| `saleId`
| `SaleId`
| Unique sale identifier.

| `soldBy`
| `UserId`
| User who recorded the sale.

| `occurredAt`
| `DateTime`
| Timestamp when the sale was confirmed.

| `totalAmount`
| `Money`
| Total amount calculated at creation.

| `lines`
| `List<SaleLineItem>`
| At least one line is required. Copied with `List.copyOf` at construction and exposed through `getLines()` as `List<SaleLineDto>`.
|===

== SaleLineItem attributes

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Attribute | Type | Description

| `productId`
| `ProductId`
| Product sold on this line.

| `quantity`
| `int`
| Units sold.

| `unitPrice`
| `Money`
| Snapshot of the product's unit price at the time of sale.

| `lineTotal`
| `Money`
| `quantity × unitPrice`, calculated when the line is instantiated.
|===

== Business invariants

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Code | Invariant

| R-SALE-01
| Create a sale with at least one `SaleLineInput`.

| R-SALE-02
| Each line sells at least one unit, checked in `SaleLineItem`.

| R-SALE-03
| Each captured unit price is strictly positive, checked in `SaleLineItem`.

| R-SALE-04
| `totalAmount` equals the exact sum of all `lineTotal` values.
|===

== Internal line entity

`SaleLineItem` is an internal entity with positional identity within its sale. Two lines may refer to the same product at the same price, for example following an adjustment.
A line cannot be queried, changed or referenced independently of its `Sale`.

== Relationship to StockMovement

The sale triggers stock movements but does not contain them.

----

A sale line records "four filters sold at €10 each". The stock movements record "three units from the shop floor and one from backstock".

== Daily revenue

Daily revenue is a calculated view: filter sales by `occurredAt` for the day and sum their `totalAmount` values.
A repository or read model handles this query.
